OpenT1 is a T1 product with Faker at its heart — not a generic esports app. The Alarm: official announcements the moment they land, fixtures and results converted to each fan's own timezone, Faker milestones. Truth labels for fans: official announcements clearly separated from rumour (官方 / 傳聞 / 未證實). And the Forum (應援區) — the best fan forum in it — multilingual from day one, because T1's fandom is global.
